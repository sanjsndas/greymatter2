    Contact & Submission FAQ
    How do I pitch an article to Grey Matter Homes? +
    Send a concise pitch to pitches@greymatterhomes.com with the subject line "Pitch: [Topic]". Include a working title, a 2–3 paragraph summary, and a short bio. We welcome perspectives from architects, designers, researchers, and experienced writers. We typically respond within 5–7 days.
    Do you accept guest posts or sponsored content? +
    We occasionally accept carefully vetted sponsored content that aligns with our editorial standards. All sponsored content is clearly labeled. For rates and guidelines, contact ads@greymatterhomes.com.
    Can I republish an article from your site? +
    Unless otherwise noted, all content is copyrighted. You may quote up to 100 words with a link back to the original article. For full republishing requests, please email editor@greymatterhomes.com.
    I have a product or material to suggest for review. Where do I send it? +
    We're always looking for innovative materials and products. Please send a brief description and high-resolution images to editor@greymatterhomes.com with "Product submission" in the subject line. Due to volume, we cannot guarantee coverage.
