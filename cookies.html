    Grey Matter Homes ("we", "us", "our") operates the website www.greymatterhomes.com (the "Site"). This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can manage your preferences. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

    In short: We use essential cookies to make the Site work, and with your consent we use analytics and preference cookies to understand how you interact with our content. You can change your cookie settings at any time via our cookie banner or your browser settings.

    1. What Are Cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to provide a better user experience, and to give website owners information about how their site is being used.

    Cookies do not typically contain any information that personally identifies a user, but personal information that we store about you may be linked to the information stored in and obtained from cookies.

    1.1 Types of cookies by duration

    • Session cookies: These are temporary cookies that remain in your browser's cookie file until you leave the Site. They are essential for certain features to work, such as navigating between pages efficiently.
    • Persistent cookies: These remain in your browser's cookie file for a set period (or until you delete them). They are used to remember your preferences and actions across multiple visits.

    1.2 Types of cookies by source

    • First-party cookies: Set by us directly on the Site.
    • Third-party cookies: Set by trusted third-party services we use (e.g., analytics providers) to help us understand Site usage and improve our content.

    2. How We Use Cookies

    We use cookies for the following purposes:

    Category Purpose Examples
    Essential These cookies are necessary for the Site to function correctly. They enable core functionality such as page navigation, security, and access to protected areas. The Site cannot function properly without these cookies. Session management, CSRF protection, load balancing.
    Analytics These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the Site by collecting and reporting information anonymously. We use this data to improve our content, layout, and user experience. Google Analytics (anonymised IP), page views, time on page, bounce rate.
    Preferences These cookies allow the Site to remember choices you make (such as your language or region) and provide enhanced, more personal features. Remembering your cookie consent choice, display preferences.
    Marketing We do not currently use marketing or advertising cookies. Should this change, we will update this policy and request your consent. —

    We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information, and we never sell cookie data to third parties.

    5. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

    You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies (other than essential cookies). You can exercise your cookie preferences in the following ways:

    5.1 Cookie consent banner

    When you first visit the Site, you will see a cookie banner that allows you to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer or by clearing your browser cookies and refreshing the page.

    5.2 Browser settings

    Most web browsers allow some control of most cookies through the browser settings. To find out more about cookies, including how to see what cookies have been set and how to manage and delete them, visit www.allaboutcookies.org.

    Please note that if you disable or refuse cookies, some parts of the Site may become inaccessible or not function properly.

    5.3 Opt-out of analytics

    You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

    6. Do Not Track Signals

    Some browsers support a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature that sends a signal to websites indicating you do not wish to be tracked. Our Site does not currently respond to DNT signals. However, you can manage your preferences through our cookie consent banner and browser settings as described above.
