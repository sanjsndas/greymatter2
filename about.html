We believe good design begins with curiosity.

Grey Matter Homes is an independent editorial journal for people who think deeply about the spaces they inhabit — and the materials, light, and structure that shape them.

Independent & Ad-Free

We are not beholden to advertisers or brands. Our recommendations are based on research, experience, and a genuine desire to help our readers make better design decisions.

Rigorous & Practical

Every article is grounded in real-world application — from material science to space planning. We combine editorial depth with actionable guidance you can use.

Timeless over Trendy

We cover trends, but we prioritize principles. Our goal is to help you create spaces that endure — functionally, aesthetically, and environmentally.

Our Editorial Principles

These are the standards that guide every article, review, and guide we publish.

  • Accuracy first. We fact-check, cite sources, and correct errors promptly and transparently.
  • No pay-for-play. We do not accept payment for coverage. Sponsored content is clearly labelled.
  • Real-world testing. Where possible, we test materials, visit projects, and interview practitioners.
  • Inclusive design. We believe good design should serve diverse bodies, budgets, and ways of living.
  • Sustainability matters. We evaluate the environmental impact of materials and methods — not just their look.
  • Reader trust. Your attention is valuable. We aim to earn it with every article we publish.
